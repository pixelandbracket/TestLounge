Questions

Before you convert.

Do my files get uploaded?

No. ConvertSave is designed around local conversion, so your source files can be processed on your own computer instead of uploaded to a cloud conversion service.

Which computers does it work on?

ConvertSave is built for macOS, Windows, and Linux.

How many devices can I use?

A ConvertSave license can be active on up to three devices.

Can I convert more than one file?

Yes. The product is designed around a queue so you can work through batches and mixed file types without starting over for every file.
